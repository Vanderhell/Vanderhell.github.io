Why LOX exists

Solve recurring embedded problems once.

Embedded products repeatedly rebuild the same critical foundations. LOX turns those foundations into focused, reusable libraries with explicit ownership and failure contracts.

01Persistent stateKeep configuration and telemetry across restarts.
02RecoveryResume safely after interruption or power loss.
03Runtime decisionsMake budgets, permissions and constraints explicit.
04DiagnosticsPreserve the evidence needed to understand a failure.
